Google API Services & Limited Use Disclosure

Wheedle Technologies uses Google APIs and Google OAuth only to provide functionality requested and authorized by our users.

Our access, use, storage, processing, and transfer of information obtained from Google APIs is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that the user has requested or authorized.

We do not sell Google user data, transfer it to data brokers, use it to build unrelated advertising profiles, or use Google authentication data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

When a user explicitly asks Wheedle to create, manage, publish, analyze, or optimize advertising campaigns through an authorized Google advertising account, Google-related advertising data is processed only as necessary to provide those requested campaign-management features.

For more information, see the Google API Services User Data Policy .

1. About Wheedle Technologies

Wheedle Technologies is an AI-powered digital marketing and campaign management platform designed to help businesses create, manage, publish, monitor, analyze, and optimize digital advertising campaigns.

Depending on the services enabled by a user, Wheedle may allow users to connect advertising and business accounts from platforms such as:

  • Google
  • Meta / Facebook
  • Instagram
  • LinkedIn
  • X and other supported advertising platforms

Wheedle may also analyze website or business information submitted by users to help generate marketing content, campaign suggestions, advertising creatives, targeting recommendations, and campaign strategies.

Users may connect supported third-party accounts so that Wheedle can perform advertising actions directly on those platforms only after receiving the user's authorization.

Wheedle Technologies may provide users with the option to sign in, register, or connect their account using Google OAuth / Google Sign-In.

When a user chooses to sign in using Google, we may receive information authorized by the user and provided by Google.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

We use this information primarily to:

4. Google Ads and Google API Data

Users may choose to connect an authorized Google advertising account to Wheedle Technologies in order to use digital advertising and campaign-management functionality.

Access to Google advertising information occurs only after the user completes Google's authorization process and grants the permissions required for the selected feature.

Depending on the permissions authorized by the user and the features being used, Wheedle may access or process information such as:

  • Google Ads customer or advertising account identifiers
  • Authorized advertising account information
  • Campaign information
  • Ad group information
  • Advertising creatives and assets
  • Campaign status
  • Campaign objectives
  • Budget and bidding settings
  • Targeting configuration
  • Keywords or related campaign settings
  • Campaign performance metrics
  • Clicks, impressions, conversions, and related reporting data
  • Other Google Ads information authorized by the user

4.1 How Google Advertising Data Is Used

Google advertising information may be used to provide user-requested functionality such as:

  • Displaying connected advertising accounts
  • Creating advertising campaigns
  • Creating or updating advertising creatives
  • Managing campaign settings
  • Publishing campaigns authorized by the user
  • Pausing, updating, or managing campaigns
  • Displaying campaign analytics
  • Generating campaign reports
  • Providing campaign performance insights
  • Providing AI-assisted optimization recommendations

Wheedle does not independently publish, modify, or manage a user's Google advertising campaigns without an action, configuration, permission, or authorization provided by the user through the platform.

4.2 Google OAuth Tokens

Where necessary to maintain an authorized Google connection, Wheedle may securely process authentication or authorization tokens provided through Google's OAuth system.

Such credentials are used only to communicate with authorized Google services and to perform functionality requested by the connected user.

Users may disconnect their Google integration or revoke Wheedle's Google access through applicable Wheedle account controls or through their Google Account settings.
